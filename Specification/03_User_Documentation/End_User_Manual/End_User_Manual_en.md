# End-User Manual

**Product:** Virtual Foreign Language (VFLS) · **Version:** 1.0
**Audience:** Learners using the app on Android or Windows.

---

## 1. Welcome

Virtual Foreign Language helps you practise English by holding
conversations with AI tutor characters. You can chat freely or follow
guided role-play scenarios, and you receive feedback on your speaking
skills after every conversation.

## 2. Getting started

### 2.1 Install and open

Install the app on your Android device or Windows PC and open it. The
splash screen appears while the app prepares.

### 2.2 Create an account

On first use, tap **Sign up** and provide the requested details. Choose
a password of at least 6 characters. If you already have an account,
tap **Sign in**. You may choose to have the app remember your account
for faster sign-in next time.

### 2.3 Onboarding

After your first sign-in, a short onboarding introduces the app. It
appears only once.

## 3. The main screens

The app has five main tabs:

| Tab | Purpose |
|-----|---------|
| **Home** | Your starting point — recommendations and quick actions |
| **Scenarios** | Browse role-play exercises by category and difficulty |
| **History** | Your past conversations |
| **Progress** | Your skills, streak, level, and achievements |
| **Settings** | Account, appearance, and preferences |

## 4. Having a conversation

### 4.1 Start a conversation

1. Choose a **tutor persona** — each has a different accent and style.
2. Optionally pick a **scenario** for a structured role-play; without
   one, you have a free conversation.
3. Choose a mode:
   - **Chat** — text conversation.
   - **Face** — talk with an animated tutor avatar.

### 4.2 During the conversation

- Type your message, or use the microphone for speech (see §5).
- The tutor replies. Continue the conversation naturally.
- If you are unsure what to say, request a **suggestion**.
- Tap **End** when you are finished.

### 4.3 After the conversation

You receive a **session report** with:

- An overall score plus per-skill scores (pronunciation, fluency,
  vocabulary, grammar, engagement, listening).
- Your strengths and areas to improve.
- XP earned.

## 5. Speaking with your voice

The app supports speech input and spoken tutor replies. Voice features
require a **speech model** to be installed on your device.

- If no model is installed when you start a speaking conversation, the
  app guides you to a **setup screen**.
- You may continue in **text-only** mode if you prefer.
- Grant the microphone permission when asked, so the app can hear you.

## 6. Progress, streaks, and achievements

- **XP and level** — you earn XP for activity; XP raises your level.
- **Streak** — practising on consecutive days builds your streak.
- **Skills** — the Progress tab shows how your skills trend over time.
- **Achievements** — unlock achievements by reaching milestones.

## 7. Scenarios and courses

- **Scenarios** are individual role-play exercises with objectives and
  key phrases. Each shows an estimated time and an XP reward.
- **Courses** group scenarios into an ordered learning path.
- Completing a scenario records your best score.

## 8. News

The News section shows announcements and updates. Unread posts are
indicated; you can mark posts as read.

## 9. Settings

From the Settings tab you can:

- Edit your **profile** — display name, avatar, gender, language.
- Change your **password** (you must enter your current password).
- Choose a **theme**, **font**, and chat-bubble style.
- Set your default conversation mode.

## 10. Working offline

If your device loses connectivity, the app still shows previously
loaded content (scenarios, history, progress) from its local cache.
Actions that need the network — starting a conversation, sending a
message — require a connection. The app shows a clear message rather
than a technical error when you are offline.

## 11. Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---------|-----------|
| Cannot sign in | Check your credentials and network; passwords are case-sensitive |
| "Speech model not installed" | Follow the setup screen, or continue in text-only mode |
| Microphone not working | Grant the microphone permission in your device settings |
| Conversation seems slow | The app may be waiting on the network; try again on a better connection |
| App shows cached/old data | You may be offline; reconnect to refresh |

## 12. Privacy note

Your conversations are processed to provide tutoring and scoring.
Manage your account and preferences from the Settings tab.
